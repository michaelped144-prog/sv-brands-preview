Before the live

Quick answers to the most common questions

  • How long is the training?

    Plan for ~90 minutes. We'll walk through the entire Stay Viral System live and leave time at the end for Q&A. Stay until the end for a chance to win a fully-loaded TikTok Shop account.

  • Do I need followers or experience?

    No. The system works for brand-new accounts with zero followers. Most of our students start from scratch — we'll show you the exact setup, product picks, and hooks that get videos to spike.

  • Does this cost anything to get started?

    TikTok Shop is free to apply to, and you don't need a budget for ads. We'll walk through which products to start with (most are free to sample once you're approved) and how the commission side works.

  • What if I'm outside the US?

    TikTok Shop is live in the US, UK, and several other markets. The system applies anywhere the platform is — we'll cover what's different for international creators during the training.

  • Will the training be recorded?

    If something comes up, we'll text you a replay link afterward. But the Q&A, the bonuses and the giveaway only happen live, so block the time and treat it like a meeting you can't miss.

  • I'm slammed with my day job — can I still do this?

    Yes. Most of our students run this on the side of a full-time job (60–90 minutes a day). We'll show you the exact daily workflow and how to outsource it once you have proof of concept.
