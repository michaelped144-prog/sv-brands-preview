FAQ

Common questions.

  • Where do I find my course?
    Your course lives in the app. Download it on iOS or Android, sign in with the email you bought with, and it unlocks automatically in the Learn tab.
  • What creator tools are in the app?
    The Create tab has Daily virals (yesterday’s top-selling TikTok Shop videos, with Make my version), an AI script writer, a teleprompter that scrolls your script under the camera, an AI video editor (auto cut, auto captions, voiceover) and a video coach: paste a TikTok link or pick a draft and get a score, what worked and what to fix.
  • What is SV AI?
    SV AI is your success manager inside the app. It checks in, helps you connect your TikTok Shop, answers questions like which products to promote, and can get you to the team.
  • How does Scale work?
    Scale gives you a daily focus, a streak, a weekly video target and a roadmap of chapters and missions with XP. Badges come from your verified TikTok Shop sales, and you can see where you rank on the leaderboard.
  • Do I need a TikTok Shop account?
    Yes. The app plugs into TikTok Shop to add products to your showcase and track payouts. Setup takes about 2 minutes.
  • How do payouts work?
    We surface every commission via our Growi integration. Money still pays out on TikTok Shop's normal schedule — we just give you one dashboard to see it all.
  • When are new brand deals added?
    Daily. Our team vets every brand before it hits the feed, so what you see is approved and ready to ship.
  • What if my account hits a strike or ban?
    As an official TikTok agency partner, we have direct lines to resolve violations. Coaching members get full ban support — app users have community help.
  • Is the app US-only?
    Right now, yes — TikTok Shop limitation. We'll expand as TikTok rolls out new regions.
