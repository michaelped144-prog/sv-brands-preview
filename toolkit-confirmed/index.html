  1. 01

    Create your free Base44 account

    Clone the tool kit

    Locked Done

    Open the template and hit Use Template. The whole tool kit copies into your own Base44 workspace — yours to keep, yours to change.

    Open the template

    Complete Step 01 to unlock

    Template not opening yet? Use the hosted tool kit meanwhile.

  3. 03

    Publish your copy

    Locked Done

    In your new copy, open Dashboard → Settings and hit Publish. That gives you your own live URL. Bookmark it — that link is your daily workspace from here on.

  4. 04

    Sign up and start scripting

    Locked Done

    Create your account inside your freshly published app, run the 60-second onboarding — display name, niches, daily target — then head to the Posts tab and script your first video.
