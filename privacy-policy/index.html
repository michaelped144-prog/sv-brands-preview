1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Site, make a purchase, subscribe to a newsletter, fill out a form, submit a lead generation form (including forms on third-party platforms such as TikTok and Facebook), or otherwise contact us. When you submit a lead form through our advertising on TikTok, Meta, or other platforms, your information is shared with us by that platform for lead verification, follow-up, and optimization purposes. This information may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing and payment information (processed securely through our third-party payment processor; we do not store full credit card numbers)
  • Mailing address
  • Any other information you choose to provide

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and your visit, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URLs and exit pages
  • Pages viewed and time spent on pages
  • Click-through data
  • Date and time of your visit
  • Device type (mobile, desktop, tablet)

1.3 Information Collected Through Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, pixel tags, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activity. Specifically, we use:

  • Meta Pixel (Facebook Pixel): We use the Meta Pixel to measure the effectiveness of our advertising on Facebook and Instagram, deliver targeted advertisements, and build custom audiences for future advertising. The Meta Pixel places a cookie on your browser that may persist for up to 90 days and collects data about your activity on our Site, which is shared with Meta Platforms, Inc. For more information, see Meta’s Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok Pixel: We use the TikTok Pixel to track conversions from TikTok ads, optimize ads, and build targeted audiences for future ads. The TikTok Pixel collects data about your activity on our Site and shares it with TikTok (ByteDance Ltd.). For more information, see TikTok’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google/YouTube Tags (Google Ads, Google Analytics 4): We use Google tracking tags and Google Analytics 4 to measure the effectiveness of our advertising on Google Search and YouTube, analyze website traffic, and deliver targeted advertisements. Google Analytics 4 automatically collects user behavior data including page views, scrolls, and clicks, and retains user-level data for up to 14 months. This data is shared with Google LLC and may be used across Google services. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Cookies: Small data files placed on your device that help us improve your experience, understand Site usage, and deliver relevant advertising. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. We may use both session cookies (which expire when you close your browser) and persistent cookies (which remain on your device until deleted or expired).

  • To monitor and analyze usage trends and improve the Site
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud and technical issues

4. Your Privacy Rights

4.1 California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know: You have the right to request that we disclose what personal information we have collected, used, disclosed, and sold about you in the past 12 months.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request that we delete the personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: You have the right to request that we correct inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing: You have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. While we do not sell personal information for monetary consideration, the use of tracking technologies for targeted advertising may constitute “sharing” under the CPRA. You can opt out by contacting us at support@stayviral.io.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@stayviral.io. We will respond to verifiable consumer requests within 45 days.

Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws (including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and others) may have similar rights to access, delete, correct, and opt out of certain data processing. To exercise your rights, please contact us at support@stayviral.io.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you may have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, and to object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@stayviral.io.

5. Opt-Out of Marketing Communications

You may opt out of receiving marketing emails from us by clicking the “unsubscribe” link at the bottom of any marketing email, or by contacting us at support@stayviral.io. Please note that even if you opt out of marketing emails, we may still send you transactional or account-related communications.

You can opt out of targeted advertising from our advertising partners through the following methods:

When you first visit our Site, you may be presented with a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by:
