Before we meet

Quick answers to common questions

How long is the call? +

Plan for 20-30 minutes. We move fast — most calls hit the deciding question by the 15-minute mark, and we'll either wrap or go deeper from there based on fit.

What should I have ready? +

Your current TikTok numbers (followers, views, what you're earning), your real goal (the income or outcome you actually want), and your biggest blocker right now. We map a plan around what's real — not what's aspirational.

Will you try to pitch me on the call? +

Only if Inner Circle is genuinely a fit. We don't open with a pitch — we audit where you are first. If the math doesn't work for you, we'll tell you straight and save us both the time. If it does work, we'll walk you through exactly what we'd build together.

What if I need to reschedule? +

Use the link in your Calendly confirmation email — takes 10 seconds. Just don't no-show without rescheduling first; it makes it harder for the team to give you another slot.

Who will I be speaking with? +

Someone on the Stay Viral team — typically a senior coach who actually runs the system themselves. Not a junior SDR reading from a script.

What if Inner Circle isn't a fit for me? +

We'll tell you. Often it's a timing thing (income runway, focus availability) or a product-market thing (TikTok Shop might not be the best platform for what you're building). Either way you'll walk off the call with a clearer picture of your fastest path forward — not a confused 'no' you don't understand.
