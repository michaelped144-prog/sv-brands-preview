Questions

Questions

  • What is the difference between managed and self-serve? +

    Self-serve is $697/month. Your team runs the creator program in the workspace at campaigns.stayviral.io. Fully managed is a custom quote. Stay Viral recruits, onboards, manages agreements and delivery, runs the campaign, tracks content, reports, and handles payout operations. You approve, and you watch the same workspace.

  • What results should I expect? +

    The figures on this page are tracked GMV from brand partnerships. Selected partnership results · Tracked GMV. Past results do not guarantee future performance. The $50M+ figure is the total across brand partnerships, not one brand. Goli, Medicube, Dude Wipes, and My Peeps are shown by logo.

  • How do you find creators? +

    Through invitations, applications, and an opt-in marketplace. Creators choose whether to apply. Nothing enrolls a creator automatically. You decide who is accepted. A marketplace screen is not shown.

  • How do samples work? +

    The workspace tracks sample requests and where they stand: requested, awaiting shipment, or in transit. It does not buy postage, print a label, or hand a parcel to a carrier.

  • Is there a contract or commitment? +

    Commitment and contract terms are discussed on the call. This page does not publish a term length.

  • What does the monthly price leave out? +

    The monthly fee is the operating system. Creator retainers, commissions, and product samples are separate. Adding a payment method does not fund a creator budget.

  • How do payouts work? +

    The workspace keeps the payment record. It does not silently send money. A person confirms the payout. There is no stored-value wallet and no automatic top-up.

  • Can it message creators on TikTok? +

    Invitations can go through Stay Viral and email. General live sending is disabled. The workspace does not send those follow-ups as an automatic email or TikTok message. Native TikTok collaboration is a controlled rollout with search, preview, and receipts.

  • What am I looking at in the product shots? +

    Actual dashboard · Identifying and individual payment details redacted Campaign totals, sample counts, delivery progress, and reporting coverage notices stay visible.
